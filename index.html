Part 1 · Reading

Lee y elige A, B o C.

SCHOOL LIBRARY: Please return all books before Friday. New books arrive on Monday.

1. What should students do before Friday?

Email from Anna: Hi Sam, I can't meet at 3. Can we meet at 4 at the café? Anna

2. What time will they meet?

NOTICE: Football practice is cancelled today because of rain. Practice will be on Thursday at 5.

3. Why is football practice cancelled?

Text from Tom: Mum, I'm going to the supermarket to buy milk and bread. I'll be back at 6. Tom

4. What is Tom going to buy?

SIGN: No food or drink in the computer room.

5. What is not allowed in the computer room?

Part 2 · Reading – Matching

Escribe el club correcto: A, B o C.

Club A – Music Club: Tuesdays, 4–5 p.m. Learn guitar and sing. Bring your own instrument.

Club B – Art Club: Wednesdays, 4–5 p.m. Drawing and painting. All materials provided.

Club C – Science Club: Fridays, 4–5 p.m. Fun experiments. No equipment needed.

1. Maria wants to paint, but she doesn't have any paints.

2. John has a guitar and wants to learn songs.

3. Emma likes doing experiments.

4. David wants an activity on Friday.

5. Lucy enjoys drawing and can go on Wednesday.

Part 6 · Writing

Task 1 – Email

You are going to visit a new city next weekend. Write an email to a friend. Say where you are going, who you are going with, and ask your friend to come. Write 35–50 words.

Task 2 – Short story

Write a short story about a day when you found something unusual. Write 35–50 words.

Part 7 · Speaking

Escribe notas para responder oralmente.

2. Describe your favourite place.
